How GPA is calculated

  1. 1

    Turn grades into points

    Plus and minus shift a grade by 0.3. For weighted GPA, Honors adds 0.5 and AP adds 1.0 first.

    A4.0B3.0C2.0D1.0F0
  2. 2

    Multiply by credits

    A 3.0 in a 2-credit class counts as 6.0 points.

  3. 3

    Add up and divide

    Add the points from every class, then divide by your total credits.

Example

A in a 1-credit class, B in a 1-credit class, A- in a 2-credit class.

(4.0 × 1 + 3.0 × 1 + 3.7 × 2) ÷ 4 = 3.60

Changing Out of rescales the result, so a 3.60 on the default 4.0 scale becomes 90 out of 100.

Schools differ, so check how yours calculates GPA.
